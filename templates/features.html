This is the operational engine where advocates and support teams prepare filings, manage evidence, and coordinate hearing readiness.

  • Inputs: active matters, uploaded files, hearing schedules, team assignments.
  • Core functions: document version control, tagged evidence indexing, task dependencies, deadline escalations.
  • Outputs: hearing-ready files, completion logs, status updates for internal and client use.

This module converts legal work into measurable financial outcomes while preserving billing transparency and accountability.
