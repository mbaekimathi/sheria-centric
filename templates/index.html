FAQ

Questions firms ask us

Is my firm’s data secure?

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). We run daily backups, role-based access control and a full audit trail on every action.

Can we import our existing matters and documents?

Yes. Our onboarding team helps you bulk-import matters, clients and documents from spreadsheets or your current system. Most firms are fully live within 2–3 weeks.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. SHERIA CENTRIC is a responsive web app that works on any modern phone or tablet — ideal for advocates checking matters in court.

Is it compliant with LSK and East African regulations?

The platform is built around LSK practice standards and supports compliance reporting for Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania.

What if we need to cancel?

Cancel any time from your billing settings. You keep access until the end of your billing period and can export all your data on the way out.
