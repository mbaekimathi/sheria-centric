Pricing FAQ

Common billing questions

Is there really a free trial?

Yes. Every plan includes a 30-day free trial with full access to the features in your chosen tier. No credit card required to start.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Absolutely. Switch anytime from your billing settings. Annual plans save the equivalent of two months compared to monthly billing.

What happens when I add more users?

Additional users are charged at your plan’s per-user rate, prorated for the remainder of your billing period. If you outgrow a tier, we’ll help you upgrade smoothly.

Do you charge for data migration?

Starter and Professional plans include guided onboarding. Enterprise Firm plans include a dedicated migration team for bulk matter, client, and document imports.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel anytime. You keep access until the end of your billing period and can export all your data before your account closes.
